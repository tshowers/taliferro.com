The real problem

Most growth plans fail because the store can’t hold the pressure

Growth doesn’t just increase sales. It increases complexity: more SKUs, more promos, more channels, more returns, more customer expectations. If the platform is fragile, growth amplifies the cracks.

  • Performance debt — mobile speed drops as scripts and apps pile up.
  • Offer drift — discounts and rules conflict and create confusion.
  • Measurement gaps — attribution gets noisy right when spend increases.
  • Operational drag — fulfillment and support issues erode trust.

FAQ

Common questions about growth work

What’s the fastest way to grow an e-commerce store?

Most stores grow fastest by reducing leakage: improve mobile speed, clarify product pages, remove checkout friction, and fix tracking so you can invest in what works.

Do you focus on conversion or marketing?

We focus on the system that converts traffic into revenue: site performance, UX clarity, checkout reliability, and measurement. We can support marketing teams by making the store easier to convert and easier to measure.

How do you increase average order value (AOV)?

We improve AOV with simple offer mechanics: bundles, thresholds, add-ons, and clearer value framing— then we measure lift and keep what actually works.

Can you help with retention?

Yes. We help improve retention with post-purchase flows, email and SMS triggers, account UX, and reducing operational mistakes that create refunds and churn.
