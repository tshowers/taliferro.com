Social Media Outreach

Social should build audience, not create more busywork

TODD helps turn offers, documents, campaign context, and business observations into platform-aware social drafts. It is not trying to make every post sound the same. Each platform has its own rhythm.

  • LinkedIn for professional insight and credibility.
  • Threads for short, conversational momentum.
  • Bluesky for clean, compact posts.
  • Reddit for useful, specific, non-sales conversation.
  • YouTube when a real video asset is attached.
  • Google Business Profile for useful local or business updates when the destination is configured.

Built for signal, not spam

TODD Social writes toward a real audience, not a generic content calendar.

Drafts can support campaigns, offers, education, community-building, and the time-back story without turning every post into a product pitch.

You still approve what matters. TODD helps prepare the words, platform fit, and next move.

FAQ

Questions people ask about Outreach

Can I explore Outreach before sending is enabled?

Yes. You can inspect the workflow, draft messages, and understand the feature before provisioning is complete.

Why are send and queue sometimes disabled?

Outreach respects provisioning. Sending stays locked until the email setup is ready, so the workflow stays safe and intentional.

What is Catalyst?

Catalyst is the part of Outreach that helps turn email response into the next action. It keeps replies from becoming dead ends.

Is this just an email sender?

No. Outreach is about draft, campaign flow, engagement, performance, and what to do with response after the message lands.

Does Outreach include social media?

Yes. TODD Outreach includes social media draft generation for platform-aware posts. It supports audience-building, campaign support, offer context, and practical business commentary instead of generic content.
