Client Background

A high-volume communication environment needed a better front line

The client operated in a large telecommunications environment where customers relied on phone and text channels for billing questions, technical support, plan details, account updates, and service issues.

Even with self-service tools in place, a large share of customer communication still flowed through calls and text messages. Support teams handled repetitive questions alongside complex problems, which created delays and inconsistent customer experiences.

Taliferro Group was engaged to integrate AI-powered automation into business texting and phone interactions using a Custom GPT solution. The goal was not to remove human support. The goal was to let AI handle routine work while human agents focused on issues that required judgment.

Challenges

The existing system relied too heavily on manual response handling

High inquiry volume, repeated questions, and inconsistent context made customer communication slower than it needed to be.

  1. High call and text volume Thousands of daily inquiries included many simple, repetitive questions about billing, service status, plan details, and support next steps.
  2. Manual responses slowed operations Agents had to respond personally to routine calls and texts, which increased workload and made it harder to focus on complex cases.
  3. Limited AI-driven automation Existing automation depended on fixed templates rather than natural language understanding, which limited usefulness and response quality.
  4. Inconsistent customer experience Different agents gave different levels of information, and customers often had to repeat themselves after transfers.
  5. No seamless AI-human handoff The system needed smarter escalation, better routing, and useful summaries for agents who took over from AI.
Approach

The solution connected AI, APIs, routing, and human support

Taliferro designed the communication layer so AI could respond naturally, retrieve accurate information, and escalate when the conversation needed a person.

Automation

Routine calls and texts moved to AI

AI handled frequently asked questions, basic troubleshooting, billing updates, plan details, and routine text responses.

  • Reduced wait time.
  • Answered common questions instantly.
  • Freed agents for complex cases.
NLP

Responses became more natural

Natural language processing helped the AI understand customer intent, generate human-like responses, and preserve context across interactions.

  • Improved response quality.
  • Reduced repeated explanations.
  • Supported more useful handoffs.
Integration

APIs connected AI to real information

Custom API integrations and webhooks connected the AI system to support tools, knowledge base content, and account data.

  • Improved answer accuracy.
  • Reduced stale information.
  • Supported real-time response quality.
Escalation

Human agents got better context

AI routed complex issues to the right department and produced summaries so agents could start with context instead of asking customers to repeat themselves.

  • Smarter routing.
  • Shorter resolution paths.
  • Cleaner AI-human collaboration.
Collaboration

The work required infrastructure alignment, not just an AI model

Taliferro collaborated with Wipro teams to align the AI solution with existing infrastructure, security expectations, engineering workflows, and support operations.

Quality Control

Testing protected the customer experience

The implementation included phased deployment, knowledge base integration, historical interaction training, live response testing, quality assurance, and failover paths for human escalation.

Lessons Learned

AI works best when it supports humans instead of pretending to replace them

The strongest results came from combining automation with reliable routing, real-time data, and continuous improvement.

  1. AI must be tailored to the business use case. The system needed customer-specific training, policy alignment, and response behavior that matched the support environment.
  2. Real-time data access improves accuracy. API calls and webhooks gave AI access to current information, which reduced misinformation and improved customer trust.
  3. Hybrid AI-human support delivers the best experience. AI handled repetitive work while agents focused on issues that required judgment, nuance, or escalation.
  4. Continuous optimization is required. AI response quality improved through feedback loops, monitoring, testing, and ongoing training from real interactions.
  5. Automation can reduce cost without lowering service quality. The system reduced repetitive workload while improving response speed and customer satisfaction.
Bottom Line

AI created a faster front line for customer communication

The system handled routine calls and texts, routed complex issues to humans, and gave agents better context when they stepped in.

Conclusion

The customer experience became more scalable

AI-driven automation reduced response times, handled routine inquiries, improved satisfaction, and made support operations more efficient without removing the human layer from complex customer care.
