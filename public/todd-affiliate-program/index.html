FAQ

Affiliate program questions

Do I need a website to join?

No. You can refer via email, social, or direct introductions using your unique code. A website helps, but it is not required.

How are referrals tracked?

We provide a unique referral code and link. Leads tied to your code are tracked and commissions are paid out after the client pays.

Are there recurring commissions?

Yes. Corporate subscriptions include a recurring percentage for the life of the contract, paid monthly.

Do I need to add disclosures?

Yes. Always disclose affiliate relationships per the FTC Endorsement Guides.

Who is this affiliate program best for?

This program fits consultants, advisors, founders, operators, and community leaders who already make warm introductions to decision-makers.

What is the difference between the affiliate program and the partner program?

The affiliate program is for making referrals and earning commissions. The partner program is for firms that want to implement TODD, deliver services, or build revenue around hands-on work.
