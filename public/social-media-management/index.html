Why We Built It

Social should support momentum, not create another pile of half-finished posts

Clients kept needing help staying consistent across channels without social publishing turning into a separate job that ate the rest of the week. The gap wasn't ideas — it was the time to reshape one idea into four different platform-appropriate drafts.

What we're not

Taliferro isn't a social media marketing agency, and this isn't a service where we post on your behalf. It's a workflow that turns your own offers and campaign ideas into drafts you review and publish yourself — social media support built into a broader execution system, not a separate specialty.

Platform-shaped, not copy-pasted

A LinkedIn post and a caption for Instagram aren't the same piece of writing wearing different hats — they need different lengths, different openings, different calls to action. The drafts reflect that instead of flattening every channel into one generic post.

Tied to a real offer

Drafts start from an actual document, offer, or campaign idea already in your workflow — not a content calendar generating posts for the sake of posting.

You publish, not us

The workflow drafts. A person reviews and posts. That's a deliberate line — the judgment about what represents your brand stays with you.
