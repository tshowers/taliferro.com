How it works

How TODD's AI actually works

TODD is not a dashboard you have to remember to check. It is a loop that runs on its own: read what changed, decide what matters, and point at the next action.

Read

TODD watches Network, Outreach, Social, Docs, Moves, and Pulse continuously — a new reply, a stalled thread, a shift in sentiment, an old contact going quiet.

Decide

It weighs what changed against what actually matters to the business right now, instead of surfacing everything with equal urgency.

Act

It drafts the outreach, raises the follow-up, or flags the risk — so a person reviews a decision instead of starting from a blank page.

The pattern is consistent

Every AI is only as good as the signal it is fed. Organizations lose momentum in familiar places: unclear next steps, weak handoffs, scattered context, and too much manual coordination.

  • An AI needs designed follow-through, not assumed follow-through.
  • Data quality is an operational issue, not just a reporting issue.
  • Workflow clarity matters as much as the model behind it.
  • AI-driven systems should reduce overhead, not add to it.

Supporting tools, not separate products

  • Signal Engine watches the outbox inside Outreach and tracks which emails are actually landing — what's hot, what's resonating — instead of guessing from open rates.
  • Lead Vault is where a team can buy a single enriched, fully-qualified lead for $29 when their own network isn't enough.

  • Assistant Box is the conversation layer — ask a question in plain English and TODD answers it from your actual contacts, notes, and tasks instead of making you hunt through dashboards.

FAQs

TODD FAQs

What is TODD?

TODD is an AI by Taliferro that helps service businesses and relationship-driven teams turn contacts, outreach, and stalled conversations into visible next steps and follow-through.

Is TODD a CRM?

No. TODD works before your system of record. It helps create consistent actions and follow-through that later get captured elsewhere.

How does TODD's AI decide what matters?

It reads signals across Network, Outreach, Social, Docs, Moves, and Pulse — a reply, a stalled thread, a shift in sentiment, a contact going quiet — and weighs them against what's actually important to the business right now, instead of treating everything as equally urgent.

How do we start?

Start by telling Taliferro Group what is stuck. If TODD is relevant, we will point you to it. If consulting, integration, or workflow cleanup is the better first move, we will tell you that instead.

Why show TODD on a consulting site?

Because it proves we build and think at the systems level. TODD is one of the ways Taliferro Tech turns AI ideas into working software, while Taliferro Group helps organizations decide what they actually need.

What parts make up TODD?

TODD includes six products: Network, Outreach, Social, Docs (which handles knowledge management), Moves, and Pulse — plus supporting tools underneath them like Signal Engine, Daily Momentum, and Lead Vault.

Where should I go if I want the product?

You can go straight to TODD at todd.taliferro.tech, or keep reading here to see how it fits into consulting work.

Can TODD help with daily revenue goals?

Yes. Daily Momentum helps turn a revenue goal into practical moves, priorities, and follow-through so the day starts from action instead of uncertainty.
