Professional Website Developer FAQs

What does a professional website developer do?

Plans, designs, and builds websites that meet business goals — from information architecture and UI to performance, SEO, analytics, and security.

How much does professional development cost?

Typical projects range from $5k–$25k depending on scope, content, and integrations. We provide a fixed estimate after discovery.

How long will it take?

Most sites launch in 3–8 weeks. Timelines vary by content readiness and approvals.

Can you improve my existing site?

Yes — audits, performance tuning, SEO fixes, accessibility improvements, and feature development are available.
