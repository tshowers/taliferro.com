What this looks like in practice

A typical fix: a service page loading in six-plus seconds on mobile, no structured data for Google to read, and a call-to-action buried under three paragraphs of stock copy. We rebuild around one clear action, get load time under two seconds, and add the schema markup that was missing. Load time and rankings are things you can verify yourself with PageSpeed Insights before and after — ask us for the same audit on your current site.

We also use AI-assisted audits to keep catching regressions after launch: automated checks that flag layout shifts (CLS), slow assets affecting LCP, form issues that hurt conversions, and content gaps.
