FAQ

Before you book

Why does IT spending keep growing without better results?

In most organizations, IT budgets grow to cover coordination overhead — meetings, re-work, context-switching, tool sprawl — not actual delivery. The work expands to fill the resources without a forcing function on output. Adding budget without fixing structure produces more expensive slowness, not speed.

How long does it take to see improvement after an IT systems intervention?

Most teams see the first measurable improvement in 2–4 weeks once blockers are clearly named and ownership is assigned. The intervention surfaces what to fix; execution is on you. The written output from this engagement is specifically designed to be actionable within days, not months.

Is slow IT delivery a technology problem or a management problem?

Almost always both. Technology complexity creates management overhead, and management overhead creates more technology workarounds. The two feed each other. Fixing one without the other doesn't hold. A good diagnostic addresses both layers.
