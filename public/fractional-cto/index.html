Fractional CTO questions we hear often

What is a fractional CTO?

A fractional CTO is a senior technology executive who works part-time or on-demand rather than as a full-time hire. You get CTO-level strategy, architecture decisions, and team direction — at a fraction of a full-time executive cost.

How much do fractional CTO services cost?

Fractional CTO services range from $5,000–$20,000/month depending on hours and scope. Taliferro also offers a $999 one-time assessment — the fastest way to see if we're the right fit before committing to an ongoing arrangement.

What's the difference between a fractional CTO and an IT consultant?

An IT consultant delivers a specific project and exits. A fractional CTO is embedded in your leadership team over time — attending strategy meetings, guiding the engineering organization, and owning technology outcomes alongside the CEO.

Do you work with companies outside Seattle?

Yes. Taliferro is based in Seattle and works with companies across the US remotely. Most fractional CTO engagements are fully remote with scheduled video calls and on-demand availability for decisions that can't wait.
