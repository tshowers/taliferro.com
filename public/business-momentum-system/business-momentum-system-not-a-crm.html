What changes

It’s not another dashboard. It’s motion

A CRM is useful once you already have process and consistency. A Momentum System helps you create that consistency in the first place.

A concrete version of this: a prospect replies to your outreach, the reply gets logged, the CRM shows an updated "last contacted" date — and nothing happens next until a person remembers to open that record again. TODD is the layer that notices the reply and puts the next move (send the follow-up, book the call, flag it as gone cold) in front of you the same day, before it's a stale record nobody's looking at.
