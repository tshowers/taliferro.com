Why partner

Why partner with Taliferro?

  • Own the client relationship — you sell, you deliver, you support.
  • Implementation revenue — keep your services revenue for APIs, UI, workflows, and integrations.
  • Product momentum — TODD is a Momentum System (MS) that helps teams move things forward— less chasing, less remembering, less busywork.
  • Taliferro backing — product support and escalation when you need it.
Partner model

Sell at retail. Keep your margin. Keep your services

  • Sell at retail — partners receive a discounted rate and keep the margin.
  • Services are yours — customization, APIs, interfaces, websites, and integrations are your revenue.
  • Not white-labeled — partners can embed and brand TODD within their offering.
  • No exclusive territories — any qualified partner can participate.
Responsibilities

What you own. What we back you on

  1. Sales & onboarding — you qualify the client and manage the relationship.
  2. Implementation — you configure TODD and deliver customization as needed.
  3. Support — you provide first-line support; Taliferro provides escalation.
Revenue

Two revenue streams. One relationship

  • Margin on TODD — you keep the difference between retail price and your partner rate.
  • Services revenue — integrations, workflows, websites, data work, and training remain yours.
  • Long-term accounts — build recurring services on top of ongoing product usage.

FAQ

Partner program FAQ

What is the TODD Partner Program?

The TODD Partner Program is for agencies and consulting teams that want to implement and customize TODD for clients. Partners sell at retail, keep their margin, and deliver services on top.

Do partners own the customer relationship?

Yes. Partners own the customer relationship and handle day-to-day support. Taliferro backs the partner with product support and escalation.

Is TODD white-labeled?

No. TODD is not white-labeled. Partners can embed and brand TODD within their own offering, while the product remains TODD.

Can partners customize TODD?

Yes. Partners can deliver customization work such as APIs, interfaces, websites, workflows, and integrations, and they keep that services revenue.

Are there exclusive territories?

No. There are no exclusive territories. Any qualified partner can participate.
