Problem Statement

While the benefits of cloud migration are widely acknowledged, executives often encounter unexpected hurdles and underexplored opportunities. These include complexities in cloud architecture, overlooked security vulnerabilities, and potential for innovative cloud applications that go beyond mere data storage and processing. Addressing these facets is critical to fully leverage the cloud’s potential and avoid pitfalls that can impede technological progress and operational efficiency.
