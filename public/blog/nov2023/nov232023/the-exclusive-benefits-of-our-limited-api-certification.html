A Differentiated Certification Process

Here’s how our certification stays credible in 2025 and avoids the pitfalls of rubber-stamp reviews:

Our certification process is not just another consultancy service. Unlike typical reviews where consultants might rush to deliver results, our approach at Taliferro Group is different:

  • Time-boxed, deep reviews. A set timeline for each certification so we’re thorough and predictable — never rushed.
  • Contracts-first, runnable checks. We lint OpenAPI/AsyncAPI, enforce naming and error taxonomies, and gate releases with contract tests.
  • Independent posture. No delivery obligations attached; our assessment is objective and defensible for leadership and auditors.

Core Elements of Our Certification

Our checklist is practical and aligned with today’s platforms and risks:

  • Zero-Trust Security. OAuth2/OIDC, mTLS, scoped tokens, secrets hygiene, and schema validation at the edge.
  • Contracts & Lifecycle. OpenAPI/AsyncAPI quality, versioning and deprecation rules, idempotency, pagination, and backward compatibility.
  • Performance & Resilience. P95/P99 targets, caching, rate limiting/back-pressure, timeouts/retries, and circuit breaking.
  • Developer Experience. Docs quality, quickstarts, SDKs, examples, consistent error models, and clear onboarding.
  • Compliance & Audit. Privacy and AI-related controls mapped to frameworks (GDPR/CCPA, NIST AI RMF); logs and audit trails.
  • Integration & Interop. REST/GraphQL/gRPC/events fit for purpose; gateway policy consistency across environments.
