Seattle's economic prowess, historically buoyed by its robust tech sector, is facing unprecedented challenges. Herein lies the perfect opportunity for the Taliferro Group to step in with its expertise in cloud technology, cybersecurity, and digital infrastructure.

Seattle’s Emerging Tech Challenges in 2025

  • Cloud Costs. Right-size, autoscale, and review unit economics monthly; adopt serverless where predictable.
  • AI Risks. Monitor bias/drift, secure data paths, and set human-in-the-loop for critical decisions.
  • API Sprawl. Enforce contracts and rate limits at the gateway; inventory shadow APIs.
  • Cyber Supply Chain. SBOMs, signed artifacts, and vendor risk reviews reduce attack surface.
  • Talent & Velocity. Paved roads (templates, CI/CD, observability) keep teams shipping safely.

Cyber-Resilient Smart City Infrastructure Framework

As Seattle moves towards becoming a smart city, ensuring the cybersecurity of its digital infrastructure is crucial. Taliferro Group offers a Cyber-Resilient Smart City Infrastructure Framework, a comprehensive solution designed to safeguard the city’s digital transformation journey.

This framework involves developing a robust cybersecurity strategy tailored to the needs of a smart city. It encompasses secure cloud architecture, real-time threat detection systems, and resilient API gateways to protect the city’s data and digital services. Unique Advantages:
