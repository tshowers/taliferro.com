We Don't Just Build Websites — We Build Revenue

People assume Taliferro is a web development shop because that's the most visible part of the work. Tyrone Showers explains why every project — a website, an API, a machine learning model — gets measured by the same standard: does it make the business more money.

TS Tyrone Showers Co-Founder Taliferro · Jul 29, 2023 · 2 min read

Introduction

When people ask what Taliferro does, they usually expect the answer "we build websites." That's true, but it undersells the actual point of the work. The real goal isn't a polished digital presence — it's making sure every technical decision we make leaves the business measurably better off financially.

A website isn't just a collection of pages — it's a tool for customer acquisition, retention, and conversion. Treated that way, it's not a cost center, it's an investment with a return that can actually be measured.

The Problem With Treating Tech as a One-Off

A lot of tech vendors treat their work as a one-time transaction: build the website, deliver the service, send the invoice, move on. That misses the actual point — every piece of technology a business invests in should be judged by whether it moves revenue, not whether it shipped on schedule.

Taliferro treats technology as part of a client's overall business strategy, not a separate function. Whether the work is performance tuning, an API integration strategy, or a machine learning model built for data mining, the standard is the same: does this leave the business in a stronger financial position than before.

Technology as a Tool, Not the Point

Take API integration as an example. Done well, it doesn't just connect systems — it removes manual work, improves the customer experience, and shows up directly in the numbers. APIs aren't a technology trend; they're a lever for the business itself.

The same logic applies to machine learning applied to data mining — most companies are sitting on revenue signals already buried in their own data, and the work is finding them, not generating new data to analyze.

The underlying approach is the same across all of it: act as a business partner focused on growth and profitability, not a vendor deploying technology for its own sake.

Conclusion

What Taliferro actually does is use technology as a revenue lever — every strategy and every piece of work is measured against whether it delivers a real financial gain, not whether it looks impressive. So the next time someone asks what Taliferro does, the honest answer isn't "they build websites" — it's "they build technology-driven financial growth."

Tyrone Showers
