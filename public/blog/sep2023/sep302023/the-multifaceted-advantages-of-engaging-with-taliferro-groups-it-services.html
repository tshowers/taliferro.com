Taliferro's IT Work Is Judged by ROI, Not Just Uptime

A project that ships on time but doesn't move revenue or cut costs wasn't worth doing. That's the standard Taliferro holds its own IT engagements to, across everything from Agile delivery to machine learning.

Published: 30 Sep 2023 · Updated: 12 Sep 2026

By Tyrone Showers

Article

Introduction

Every business wants streamlined operations and more revenue, but not every IT firm actually delivers either. Taliferro Group, based at 1424 11th Ave STE 400 in Seattle, works across value-driven solutions spanning Agile project management to machine learning — and holds all of it to the same bar: did it actually move the numbers.

Tangible Return on Investment (ROI)

The engagements that matter end with a specific answer: a new revenue stream identified, or a real operational cost cut. Clients aren't paying for effort, they're paying for a measurable outcome — either more revenue or lower costs, and ideally both.

Comprehensive Service Offerings

Agile Project Management

Agile project management means being able to adjust the plan when the market shifts mid-project, rather than sticking rigidly to a schedule that no longer fits.

Analytics

Good analytics turns raw data into decisions a business can actually act on — trends in the market, in customer behavior, in performance — instead of just producing more dashboards nobody checks.

API and Data Design

Well-designed APIs and data models let separate software systems talk to each other cleanly, which shows up directly in a smoother user experience and fewer integration headaches.

Cyber Security Services

Security work protects digital assets and lowers the odds of a breach — not a nice-to-have, a baseline requirement for doing business online today.

Machine Learning

Machine learning surfaces patterns in data that would be genuinely hard to spot by hand, and models that improve as they see more data — used well, that's a real capability, not a buzzword.

Sustainability

A real focus on sustainability isn't just good PR — it also tends to lower long-term operational risk and build trust with the people a business depends on.

Geographical Proximity

Being based in Seattle keeps Taliferro close to a real tech hub, which makes it easier to stay current on tools and practices rather than working from what was true a few years ago.

Conclusion

Taliferro Group's services span a wide range — from finding revenue hiding in existing data to hardening security — but the standard stays the same across all of them: did the work actually change something measurable for the client. That's the bar to hold any IT partner to, Taliferro included.
