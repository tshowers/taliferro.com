Nobody Buys From a Business Google Can't Find

A Google Business Profile is free, takes about ten minutes to set up, and is one of the highest-leverage things a local business can do — which is exactly why it's easy to assume it doesn't matter. It does. Here's what it actually does and what it doesn't.

TS Tyrone Showers Co-Founder Taliferro · Sep 24, 2022 · 1 min read

Introduction

Ranking well matters less than most businesses think. Getting found at all matters more. When someone searches "plumber near me" or the name of your business, a Google Business Profile is what puts you on the map — the actual map, plus the local pack of results above the regular search listings. If it's missing or incomplete, a customer who was ready to call finds a competitor instead.

What It Actually Does

It puts your business on Google Maps and in local search results, with hours, a phone number, and reviews visible before someone ever clicks through to your website. It also comes with free insights: how many people found you through search, how many called or requested directions, and what terms they searched to find you. That last part is useful — it tells you what people actually look for, not what you assumed they'd search.

What It Doesn't Do

It's not a replacement for a real marketing plan, and it won't help if people can't spell your business name correctly when they search for it. It's also not going to compensate for a website that's slow, outdated, or missing basic information once someone does click through — the profile gets someone to your door, but what Taliferro builds is what keeps them there. Think of the profile as table stakes, not a strategy.

Conclusion

It costs nothing and takes about ten minutes at google.com/business. If it's not set up, that's lost visibility for no reason at all.
