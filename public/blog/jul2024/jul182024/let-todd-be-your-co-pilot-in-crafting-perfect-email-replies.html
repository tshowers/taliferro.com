Can TODD work with my existing email setup?

Yes. TODD analyzes the content of your messages and helps you draft replies while you continue using your current email provider and tools.

No. TODD is designed to mirror your tone as closely as possible. You review every draft before sending, so you always have the final say.

No. You can choose when to let TODD draft a reply and when to write your own. TODD is there to help when your inbox feels overwhelming.
