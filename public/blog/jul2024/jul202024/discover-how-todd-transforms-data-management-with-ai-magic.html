Copy, Paste, and Relax

Here's a scenario: You're viewing a contact in TODD, and there's a section for additional information. You've just read a fascinating blog post or come across a detailed LinkedIn profile that you want to add to this contact's profile. Instead of manually entering each piece of information, you simply copy the text and paste it into TODD. Whether it's an "About" page from a website or a professional summary from LinkedIn, TODD breaks down the information and stores it in the right fields. No more tedious copying and pasting into multiple sections. TODD does it all for you, acting as your reliable co-pilot in data management.
