Emails are a critical part of our professional lives. They help us connect, engage, and build relationships. But what happens after you hit send? Do you ever wonder if your email was read, ignored, or worse, deleted? With TODD, you no longer have to guess. TODD provides you with real-time insights into your email interactions, offering a level of transparency and understanding that other tools simply can't match.

Stay Informed with Read Receipts

Picture this: You've just sent a crucial proposal to a potential client. The waiting game begins. Instead of anxiously checking your inbox for a reply, TODD keeps you informed. As soon as your email is opened, TODD sends you a read receipt. You know instantly when your message has been seen, allowing you to follow up at the perfect moment. It's like having a crystal ball for your email communications.

Track Email Deletions and Unsubscribes

ButTODDgoes beyond just read receipts. What if your email was deleted without being read? Or if the recipient decided to unsubscribe from your mailing list? TODD has you covered. When an email is deleted or an unsubscribe action is taken, TODD notifies you immediately. This level of insight allows you to refine your email strategy, ensuring your messages are not only read but also valued.
