Staff Augmentation and Consulting Solve Different Problems

Companies often reach for staff augmentation when what they actually need is consulting, or the reverse — and get frustrated when the engagement doesn't fix the problem. Taliferro breaks down what each one is actually built to solve.

TS Tyrone Showers Co-Founder Taliferro · Aug 5, 2023 · 2 min read

Introduction

Staff augmentation and consulting get lumped together constantly, and the mismatch causes real problems: a company hires a consultant expecting extra hands, or brings on contractors expecting strategic direction, and neither gets what they actually needed. The two solve genuinely different problems — here's the distinction laid out plainly.

Staff Augmentation

Staff augmentation means adding skilled people to an existing team, on a temporary basis, to execute against a plan that's already been decided. It's capacity, not direction.

Staff Augmentation: What It Actually Offers

  • Flexibility: scale the team up or down as project needs change, without a permanent headcount commitment.
  • Specialized skills: bring in specific expertise for as long as the project needs it, not longer.
  • Cost efficiency: pay for skilled work during the window it's actually needed, without the overhead of a full-time hire.

When Staff Augmentation Is the Right Call

  • A temporary spike in workload needs more hands, fast.
  • A specific skill set is needed for one project, not indefinitely.
  • The team needs to respond quickly to a market shift without a hiring cycle.

Consulting

Consulting is direction, not capacity — figuring out what the plan should be in the first place, including whether and when staff augmentation is even the right tool to execute it.

Consulting: What It Actually Offers

  • Strategic insight: a plan that's actually aligned with business goals, not just a to-do list.
  • Expert guidance: specialized knowledge and industry context brought to bear on a specific decision.
  • Problem diagnosis: identifying the actual underlying issue, not just the symptom that prompted the call.

When Consulting Is the Right Call

  • A business process needs a real diagnostic, not a quick patch.
  • Strategic planning and its implementation genuinely matter for growth.
  • An outside, unbiased perspective is needed for a decision that's too close internally to see clearly.

How They Work Together

The two aren't competitors — done well, staff augmentation and consulting are sequential, not interchangeable.

  • Strategic alignment: consulting identifies what specialized skill is actually needed, and staff augmentation supplies it.
  • Cost efficiency: consulting sets the strategy, staff augmentation executes it without the overhead of permanent hires.
  • Speed and expertise: consulting sets direction, staff augmentation brings in the right people at the right moment to move fast on it.

Conclusion

Staff augmentation and consulting aren't substitutes for each other — one supplies direction, the other supplies capacity to execute on it. Knowing which problem an organization actually has, before signing an engagement, is what determines whether staff augmentation or consulting delivers real value, or just adds cost without fixing anything.
