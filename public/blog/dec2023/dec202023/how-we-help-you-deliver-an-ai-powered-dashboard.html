FAQ

What data should go on an executive dashboard?

Only the KPIs tied to decisions: revenue, cost, throughput, risk, and delivery. If a chart doesn’t change what someone does next, it doesn’t belong.

How do you keep dashboards from becoming stale?

Assign an owner, review usage monthly, and measure whether the dashboard reduced manual reporting or improved decision speed.

What makes a dashboard “AI-powered”?

It adds signals like anomaly detection, forecasting, and recommended actions—built on trusted data and tested for accuracy.

How long does it take to deliver a first version?

Most first versions take a few weeks once KPIs and data sources are clear. The fastest wins come from starting small and iterating.
