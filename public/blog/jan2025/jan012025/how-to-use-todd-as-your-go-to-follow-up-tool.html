Why the lead actually goes cold

Nobody decides to drop a lead. It just happens in small steps: you talk to someone promising on Tuesday, mean to email them Thursday, and by the time you remember, it's been three weeks and you can't recall what you actually promised them. The deal isn't lost. It's just forgotten.

A CRM doesn't fix this. It gives you a place to log the conversation — it doesn't tell you it's time to follow up, and it definitely doesn't remember what you talked about. That part is still on you.

What TODD actually does

TODD is the follow-up layer Taliferro built on top of that gap. It doesn't just remind you a contact exists — it remembers the conversation and tells you what to do about it:

  • It surfaces who needs a nudge today, ranked by how long it's been and how warm the lead is.
  • It drafts the follow-up message itself, based on what was actually said last time.
  • It flags bad contact data — wrong email, missing phone number — before you embarrass yourself sending to it.
  • It runs the routine stuff on autopilot: a monthly check-in email to a list doesn't need you to remember it exists.

A day with TODD

Say you talked to a client last month about a project they weren't ready to start. You didn't write it down anywhere formal — it was a five-minute call. TODD keeps the thread: it knows when you last spoke, what the sticking point was, and it puts that contact back in front of you when the timing makes sense, not on some arbitrary 30-day timer.

When it does, it doesn't just say "follow up with Alex." It hands you a draft that references the actual sticking point from that call, so you're not starting from a blank page or a vague memory.

Where TODD fits with your CRM

You don't have to rip out what you already use. Most teams keep their CRM as the system of record — the place deals officially live — and run TODD in front of it as the thing that actually pushes work forward day to day. The CRM stores the history. TODD is the part that remembers to act on it.

FAQ

Can TODD handle bulk follow-ups?

Yes. TODD allows you to group contacts and set reminders for them all at once, helping you stay consistent without manual repeat work.

What happens if I miss a reminder?

TODD keeps track of missed reminders and surfaces them in your dashboard so you can catch up without losing momentum.

Is TODD suitable for small businesses?

Yes. TODD works for solo entrepreneurs and small teams, and scales as your follow-up volume grows.

If you want TODD to do the follow-up heavy lifting for you, try TODD as a Momentum System and let it turn your contacts and notes into the next moves that keep work moving.
