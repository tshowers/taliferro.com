TODD’s Assistant Box is the conversation layer of your business. It replaces dashboard hunting with real-time dialogue. You ask, TODD answers.

  • “Who works at Accenture?”
  • “When is John Smith’s birthday?”
  • “What did I say in my last email to Joe Blow?”
  • “Show me everyone I met this month.”

Context That Feels Human

If you’re viewing Accenture’s page and you type “Show me everyone here,” TODD already knows what here means. It understands context from your screen.

It’s like talking to someone in the room with you who’s paying attention.

That’s the difference between conversation and command.

Other systems wait for perfect phrasing. TODD understands intent. Whether you type “Who works at Accenture?” or “List the Accenture people,” the result is the same — quick, precise, useful.

Training TODD to Understand You

Every organization speaks its own language.

Some call deals “opportunities.” Others call them “moves.” Some use “client.” Others say “member.” TODD learns your vocabulary.

When Taliferro configures TODD for you, we train it around your words, your workflows, and your priorities.

That means when you say “Add a move,” TODD knows you mean a pipeline step — not a note or a document.

It adapts to your rhythm. You don’t adapt to it.

FAQs

Is the Assistant Box like ChatGPT?

No. ChatGPT is general. TODD is personal. It knows your contacts, tasks, and notes — and acts inside your workspace.

Can I train TODD for my own organization?

Yes. Taliferro configures TODD to understand your workflow, vocabulary, and data model.

Can TODD perform actions or only answer questions?

It does both. It can find information, create tasks, add notes, write emails, and suggest next steps.

Does TODD learn over time?

Yes. It adapts to how you phrase requests and what actions you perform most often.
