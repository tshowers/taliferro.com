EPIC Wins on Scale — Here's Where a Smaller LIS Wins Instead

Choosing a Laboratory Information System means weighing an established enterprise platform against a leaner, more adaptable one — and the right answer depends on the lab, not the vendor's size. This guide compares EPIC System and Taliferro's own LIS on features, deployment, scalability, support, and cost, so a buyer can see where each one actually fits.

TS Tyrone Showers Co-Founder Taliferro · Jun 22, 2023 · 3 min read

Abstract

This guide compares two Laboratory Information Systems (LIS) — EPIC System and Taliferro's own LIS — across features, deployment, scalability, support, and cost, so healthcare decision-makers can weigh the tradeoffs against their organization's actual requirements.

Introduction

The right LIS choice affects lab operations, workflow efficiency, and ultimately patient care, so it's worth comparing carefully. EPIC System is the established, widely-deployed player in this space; the Taliferro Group system is a newer, more adaptable alternative built for labs that want faster deployment and more customization. This guide lays out where each one actually wins.

Feature Set

Both systems cover the core LIS functions — test ordering, sample tracking, and result reporting — but they take different approaches to how that functionality is delivered:

EPIC System

  • Robust Workflow Management: a comprehensive suite for managing lab workflows, including test ordering, result validation, quality control, and integration with other healthcare systems.
  • Extensive Integration Capabilities: seamless integration with electronic health records (EHRs) and other clinical systems for data exchange.
  • Advanced Result Reporting: sophisticated reporting functionality for delivering accurate test results to providers and patients.

Taliferro Group System

End-to-End Testing Facilitation: streamlined processes covering online test ordering, sample tracking, result communication, and integration.

User-Centric Experience: intuitive interfaces, easy sample tracking, and clear communication channels.

Customization and Adaptability: built to be tailored to the specific needs and workflows of individual labs or healthcare organizations.

Deployment Options

EPIC System is designed for deep integration within an existing healthcare system's infrastructure. The Taliferro Group system offers more flexibility — rapid deployment as a complete SaaS solution, or integration within an existing lab or clinical setup. Which approach fits depends on a buyer's infrastructure, timeline, and customization needs:

EPIC System

  • Seamless Integration: designed to integrate smoothly within existing healthcare systems, including EHRs and other clinical systems, for cohesive workflows and data sharing across departments.
  • Extensive Implementation Support: implementation is typically a substantial process, requiring dedicated resources, time, and coordination with EPIC's support team.

Taliferro Group System

  • Rapid Deployment: available as a quick, complete SaaS solution — a fit for labs that want fast technological upgrades without a long implementation cycle.
  • Integration Flexibility: can also be integrated within an existing lab or clinical setup, for organizations with established infrastructure they need to work around.

Scalability

How each system scales reflects the same tradeoff seen throughout this comparison — EPIC's infrastructure built for scale versus Taliferro's flexibility to adapt:

EPIC System

  • Established Scalability: built to handle large data volumes, complex lab networks, and the needs of large healthcare organizations.
  • Accommodating Growth: provides the features and resources to expand services, add labs, and adjust workflows as an organization grows.

Taliferro Group System

  • Adaptive and Customizable: built to be tailored to labs of varying sizes and complexity, with scalability options that align with an organization's specific growth plans.

Support and Community

EPIC System has a well-established support infrastructure and a large user community that shares best practices. The Taliferro Group system takes a more direct approach — personalized support backed by hands-on lab technology expertise throughout implementation and beyond.

Cost Considerations

Cost is often the deciding factor. EPIC System, as the established industry player, typically comes with substantial upfront investment and ongoing maintenance costs. The Taliferro Group system is built around cost optimization — competitive pricing and flexible licensing aimed at organizations that don't have enterprise-scale budgets:

EPIC System

  • Initial Investment: significant upfront costs, including licensing fees, infrastructure upgrades, and dedicated resources for implementation and training.
  • Ongoing Maintenance: recurring costs for support, system updates, and any further customization.

Taliferro Group System

  • Competitive Pricing: pricing structures built for the budgets of smaller and mid-sized healthcare organizations.
  • Flexible Licensing: licensing models a buyer can match to their actual usage level and financial constraints.

The Main Difference

Who the digital experience is actually built for — internal staff, or the patient and provider on the other end of a lab result.

EPIC System

  • Digital Experience: strong internal lab management features within a healthcare system's infrastructure, streamlining internal workflows and data.
  • Limited Customer-Facing Capabilities: excels at managing internal processes, but offers less in the way of a direct, patient-facing digital experience.

Taliferro Group System

  • Enhanced Digital Experience: goes beyond internal lab management to a full customer-facing experience — online test ordering, secure result access, and direct communication channels for patients and providers.
  • Integration with Cerner/Epic: can integrate with Cerner/Epic lab testing environments, layering customer-facing functionality on top of their existing infrastructure rather than replacing it.

Conclusion

EPIC System and the Taliferro Group system solve for different priorities: EPIC for scale and deep enterprise integration, Taliferro for speed, customization, and a customer-facing experience most legacy LIS platforms don't offer. Weighing features, deployment, scalability, support, and cost against what a specific lab actually needs is what makes the choice clear.
