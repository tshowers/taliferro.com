CRMs Store Data They Don't Move It

A CRM can log every interaction and still let the reply window close, because logging isn't the same as acting. Taliferro built TODD as a Momentum System that automates the timing, drafts the follow-up, and nudges you toward the next step, instead of just giving you a place to record that you meant to.

Why CRMs miss follow-ups

Sales teams and founders know the pain: you log every interaction in the CRM, but prospects still slip through the cracks. That's because a CRM is built for storage, not for action. Manual reminders get snoozed, priorities shift, and by the time you remember, the lead has gone cold.

  • Tasks get snoozed; priorities shift; there's no daily nudge queue.
  • Context lives in inboxes and spreadsheets, not attached to a next step.
  • Inconsistent tone and duplicate emails quietly hurt trust.

How a Momentum System keeps momentum

Momentum System (MS) is the category Taliferro built TODD around: instead of just storing contacts, it makes sure every conversation gets nudged forward automatically. TODD tracks sent messages, detects replies, and schedules the next outreach step so nothing depends on someone remembering.

  1. Automated sequences with reply detection — no more sticky notes or calendar reminders.
  2. A daily nudge queue with next-best actions — see who needs attention, and when.
  3. Clean, deduped contact data linked to messages — so the follow-up references what actually happened last time.

CRM vs Momentum System

Capability CRM BMS (TODD)
Follow-up timing Manual reminders Automated sequences, paused on reply
Next-best action Often missing Daily nudge queue
Data quality Duplicates; stale fields Standardized fields, dedupe, history

Rollout checklist

  1. Define segments and messages.
  2. Build a 3-5 step sequence with timings.
  3. Connect email and contacts to TODD.
  4. Turn on reply detection; review the daily nudge queue.
  5. Measure reply rate and time-to-first-reply weekly.

FAQ

What is a Momentum System (MS)?
A BMS advances conversations with automated follow-ups, reply detection, and next-best actions so work keeps moving.
Does BMS replace my CRM?
No. Many teams keep their CRM as the system of record and use BMS for automation, timing, and nudges.
What results should I expect?
Reply-rate lift and shorter cycles when sequences and nudge queues are used consistently — the exact number depends on your list and cadence.
