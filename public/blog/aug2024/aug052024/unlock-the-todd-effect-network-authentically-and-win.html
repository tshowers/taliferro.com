Authenticity in Networking

Successful networking relies on authentic connections. Approach relationships with honesty and integrity to create a foundation of trust. This trust opens doors, leading to valuable collaborations and opportunities. In a sea of superficial interactions, authenticity sets you apart.

The TODD Effect

TODD helps you stay real without dropping the ball. Notes live next to each contact, next steps are auto-suggested, and follow-ups queue themselves. You’ll see: who you owe a reply, the last context you shared, and the best time window to reach out. That’s how casual convos become booked calls. For how this fits our Momentum System (MS), see the BMS overview. It’s a practical professional networking tool built for small business outreach and warm outreach—more relationship management than CRM.

Creating Meaningful Connections

TODD makes creating meaningful connections effortless. Reach out to contacts, follow up on previous discussions, and share relevant content. Authenticity shines through when you genuinely care about your network. Personal interactions increase the likelihood of reciprocity, leading to deeper connections.

What You’ll Notice in Week One

  • Replies climb: timely nudges on warm threads (typical +10–25% reply lift).
  • Less context switching: notes + last email preview beside each contact.
  • Fewer dropped balls: overdue follow-ups surface at the top of your day.
  • Cleaner lists: bounced or stale emails flagged for quick fixes.

FAQ

What does “authentic networking” mean?

It means showing up as yourself, listening first, and following through. Instead of blasting generic pitches, you build trust with relevant, timely outreach and keep commitments visible in one place.

How does TODD help me be more authentic?

TODD centralizes notes, preferences, and next steps so you remember context. It recommends timely follow‑ups, drafts thoughtful messages, and tracks responses—so every touch feels personal, not scripted.

Is TODD a CRM?

No. TODD is a Momentum System (MS) that does the work before a CRM—research, outreach, follow‑ups, tasks, and enrichment—so your CRM gets clean, complete data.
