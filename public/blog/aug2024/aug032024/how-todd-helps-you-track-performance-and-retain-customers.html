Track KPIs & Performance Metrics That Matter

Define and monitor the KPIs that correlate to retention and revenue: onboarding completion, time-to-first-value, engagement cadence, NPS/CSAT, renewal likelihood, and customer lifetime value (CLV). TODD surfaces at‑risk accounts and flags stalled activity before it becomes churn.

FAQ

What’s the difference between a Momentum System (MS) and a CRM?

A CRM stores records. A Momentum System (MS) drives action with proactive follow‑ups, churn alerts, and KPI dashboards.

Which KPIs should we start with?

TTFV, engagement cadence, health score, renewal likelihood, and CLV. These map directly to retention outcomes.

Do we need to migrate off our CRM?

No. You can run TODD alongside your CRM and sync contacts, activities, and outcomes.
