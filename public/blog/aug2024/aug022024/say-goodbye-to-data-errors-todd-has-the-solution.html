The Importance of Accurate Data

Accurate data drives informed decision-making. Businesses rely on data to identify trends, analyze performance, and engage customers. Inaccurate information leads to flawed strategies and wasted resources. Investing in a solution like TODD helps maintain data integrity, allowing you to focus on growth.

AI & Compliance Risks of Bad Data (2025)

Dirty inputs don’t just create annoying typos—they break AI features, misclassify contacts, and can introduce bias. In 2025, data quality is directly tied to AI performance, customer trust, and compliance (GDPR/CCPA and state privacy ventio That’s why prevention and auditability are non‑negotiable.

  • Model performance: mislabeled or duplicated entities produce weak recommendations and hallucinated insights.
  • Personalization: inconsistent fields (names, emails, addresses) lead to off‑target segmentation and messaging.
  • Bias & compliance: incomplete/incorrect attributes can skew analyses and fail audits; you need clear provenance and correction logs.
  • Revenue ops: duplicate contacts inflate pipeline and waste rep time; bad attribution derails forecasting.

Cross‑System Hygiene (multi‑CRM reality)

Most teams now run multiple systems—e.g., HubSpot for marketing, Salesforce for sales, plus outreach/event tools. TODD enforces hygiene at the edges so data stays consistent everywhere, not just in one CRM.

  • Validation at ingest: email/phone/ZIP checks, required field rules, and pattern enforcement on every import/API.
  • Global dedupe: entity matching across systems (contacts/companies) to stop duplicate leakage during syncs.
  • Normalization: canonical names/addresses and mapping tables to align fields between platforms.
  • Audit trail: who changed what and when, with exportable logs for stakeholders and compliance.
