The Project Isn't Done When It Ships — That's When the Relationship Starts

Delivery feels like the finish line, but it isn't one. The projects that turn into a second contract are the ones where someone deliberately kept the relationship alive after the invoice was paid — asked for feedback, stayed visible, made it easy to come back. Taliferro treats that discipline as part of the project, not an afterthought.

TS Tyrone Showers Co-Founder Taliferro · Mar 1, 2023 · 3 min read

Introduction

After successfully completing a project for a client, it's crucial to take specific steps that not only maintain a positive relationship but also potentially pave the way for future business opportunities. In this comprehensive guide, we'll explore these steps in detail, providing valuable insights on what to do when your project concludes.

Celebrate the Project Completion

The first and essential step is to celebrate the completion of the project. This celebration can take various forms, from a simple thank-you email to arranging a small gathering with your client and team members. By doing this, you express your gratitude for the opportunity to work together and acknowledge the collective effort that led to the project's success. Additionally, consider sharing any positive feedback received from your team as it further reinforces the sense of accomplishment.

Review and Reflect on the Project

Once the celebrations are over, it's time to review and reflect on the project. This process involves a careful analysis of what went well and what didn't. Take a close look at the successes and failures of the project, objectively assess your performance, and identify areas that could benefit from improvement. This critical self-evaluation provides valuable insights that can be used to enhance your performance in future projects.

Request Client Feedback

One of the most valuable steps in maintaining a positive client relationship is requesting feedback from the client. It's essential to know their perspective on their experience working with you and your team. Feedback can be collected through various means, such as surveys or personal phone calls. Be open to both positive and constructive feedback as it can provide invaluable insights into how you can improve your service and better align with your client's specific needs.

Express Interest in Future Opportunities

Clear communication about your interest in working with the client again is crucial. You can accomplish this by explicitly stating your desire to collaborate on future projects or by suggesting potential ideas or opportunities that might align with their needs and goals. Demonstrating your eagerness to continue the partnership sends a strong signal of commitment and professionalism.

Offer Referral Incentives

To further solidify your client relationships and expand your network, consider offering referral incentives. Encourage your satisfied clients to refer your services to others by providing them with incentives. These incentives can include discounts on future services or even a commission for every referral that ultimately becomes a client. It's a win-win situation where your clients benefit, and you gain new business opportunities through referrals.

Stay in Touch

Your relationship with the client shouldn't end once the project is completed. Staying in touch is essential to keep your company top of mind when they require services in the future. Share occasional updates on your company's progress, industry insights, or any relevant news that might be of interest to them. This consistent communication helps maintain a connection and demonstrates your ongoing commitment to providing value.

Conclusion

Successfully handling projects with clients involves a well-rounded approach that extends beyond project completion. Celebrating achievements, reviewing and reflecting on the project, requesting feedback, expressing interest in future opportunities, offering referral incentives, and staying in touch are all crucial steps in maintaining a positive client relationship and securing additional business opportunities.

By diligently following these steps, you not only enhance your professional reputation but also position yourself for long-term success in the competitive world of client-based projects. Your dedication to client satisfaction and ongoing collaboration sets you apart as a reliable partner, increasing the likelihood of continued success and growth for both you and your clients.
