Eagle Doesn't Underperform — Firms That Don't Integrate or Train On It Do

Eagle is a capable platform for trade processing and portfolio performance. Most firms that don't see the payoff aren't running into a limitation in the software — they're running it half-integrated, with a team that never got trained past the basics. Fixing that is less exciting than buying new software, and it's almost always where the real gain is.

Introduction

Efficiency in managing trade processing and portfolio performance is paramount for financial services companies. Eagle, a potent tool, offers substantial assistance in streamlining these operations and enhancing overall performance. In this article, we will delve into how financial services companies can harness Eagle's capabilities effectively for trade processing and portfolio performance.

First, let's get acquainted with Eagle. It stands as a prominent provider of investment management software solutions, encompassing investment accounting, performance measurement, and data management. The Eagle platform is meticulously crafted to provide financial services companies with an encompassing and cohesive view of their investment portfolios. By harnessing Eagle's technology, companies can attain heightened transparency in their investments, make well-informed investment choices, and augment overall performance.

So, how can financial services companies maximize the utility of Eagle for trade processing and portfolio performance? Here are some key strategies to contemplate:

Leveraging Eagle's Data Management Proficiencies

The Eagle platform boasts robust data management capabilities. It provides a centralized data repository housing a company's investment data, including transactional, market, and reference data. The advantages of these capabilities are two-fold: comprehensively viewing investment portfolios and streamlining trade processing workflows.

Automation of trade processing is a pivotal outcome, reducing error risks and enhancing efficiency. For instance, Eagle's data management capabilities can facilitate automated trade processing workflows. Additionally, these capabilities aid in reconciling trade data with custodians, ensuring data accuracy and timeliness.

Harnessing Eagle's Performance Measurement Tools

Eagle's platform offers a suite of performance measurement tools to aid in analyzing investment portfolio performance. These tools encompass performance attribution, risk analysis, and benchmarking.

By making full use of these tools, companies gain insights into the driving forces behind portfolio performance and identify areas ripe for improvement. For example, performance attribution assists in comprehending the impact of diverse investment decisions on portfolio performance. Simultaneously, risk analysis uncovers potential risk sources, enabling companies to take proactive steps in mitigating those risks.

Integration of Eagle with Other Systems

Effective integration of Eagle with other systems is another pivotal strategy. Eagle provides application programming interfaces (APIs) and integration tools that facilitate seamless connections with other systems, such as order management systems, compliance systems, and reporting systems.

This integration enhances workflow streamlining and efficiency. For example, utilizing Eagle's APIs, companies can automatically import trade data from order management systems, reducing the need for manual data entry.

Comprehensive Staff Training on Eagle's Capabilities

Equally important is ensuring that staff is thoroughly trained in utilizing Eagle's capabilities. Eagle offers training resources and support services to facilitate staff acclimatization with the platform's features and functionality.

A well-trained staff maximizes the benefits derived from Eagle. Team members proficient in Eagle are better equipped to harness its capabilities to enhance trade processing and portfolio performance effectively.

Conclusion

Eagle is an invaluable asset for financial services companies aiming to optimize trade processing and portfolio performance. Through effective utilization of Eagle's data management prowess, performance measurement tools, integration capabilities, and comprehensive staff training, companies can gain heightened visibility into their investments, make well-informed investment choices, and elevate overall performance.

The key isn't finding a better platform — it's actually using the one you have. Integration and training aren't glamorous line items, but they're the difference between Eagle as a system of record nobody trusts and Eagle as the thing that actually drives better investment decisions.
