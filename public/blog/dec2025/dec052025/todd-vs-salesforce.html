The Salesforce Problem

Salesforce assumes you already have:

  • A sales team
  • A process
  • A manager pushing people to update records
  • Someone whose full-time job is updating fields
  • Endless time to “clean data”

It doesn’t create momentum by itself. It records what your team already did.

That’s why companies end up with:

  • Dead contacts
  • Abandoned pipelines
  • Notes nobody reads
  • Reports that don’t change outcomes
  • Users who hate logging in

Salesforce isn’t bad software.
It’s just built for a world where software observes instead of assists.

TODD’s Advantage: Action Over Airtime

TODD is built around a simple belief:

If software knows the next move, it shouldn’t wait for you to make it.

TODD:

  • Writes outreach emails
  • Suggests who to contact next
  • Extracts tasks from conversations and notes
  • Analyzes data constantly in the background
  • Reacts to user behavior and changes in your pipeline
  • Creates momentum even when you’re not driving every click

This isn’t just automation.
This is intention with execution attached.

Salesforce tells you what happened.
TODD helps you make something happen.

Where Salesforce Falls Apart

Salesforce excels when:

  • You have many users
  • You have layers of approval
  • You have budget for consultants and admins
  • You need reports for leadership decks

It fails when:

  • You’re small or growing
  • You’re busy and stretched thin
  • You don’t have a sales army
  • You need action, not architecture

The world moved faster.

Most software never adjusted.

Momentum beats management, every time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is TODD a CRM?

No. TODD is a Momentum System (MS). It still stores your contacts and activity, but the focus is on writing outreach, suggesting next moves, and helping you follow through, not just logging activity.

Can TODD replace Salesforce?

It can, depending on what you use Salesforce for. Many teams keep Salesforce for reporting and use TODD for outreach, follow-ups, and next-step execution. Other teams find that once TODD runs the daily motion, the need for a traditional CRM shrinks dramatically.

Who is TODD best for?

TODD is built for busy founders, small teams, and departments that need progress more than dashboards. If you do not have a big sales operations team and you want software that actively helps you get work done, TODD is a stronger choice than a traditional CRM.
