Introducing Find
Search gives you choices.
Find gives you direction.
There is no shortage of information.
The problem is what comes next: dozens of links, competing answers, sponsored results, and another decision about where to look.
We built Find around a different idea. Instead of asking how many results we can return, Find asks how much noise we can remove.
A different approach to search
Less searching.
More finding.
Built for relevance, not volume.
01
Context first
Find considers what you mean, not simply what you typed.
02
Fewer results
Find narrows the field so you can spend less time sorting and more time deciding.
03
A clearer next step
Because finding information only matters when that information helps you move forward.
The internet became very good at giving us more.
We think the next step is knowing what to leave out.
Find is Taliferro's approach to a simpler, more contextual way of discovering information—built around relevance instead of abundance.
